Ivanov — 10–5, 67%, 57% games, 66% sets, and 3–0 at 40+ 3.5. He is the highest-rated man on his own team and 7–10 in doubles, which tells you where his captain thinks he belongs.

The ceiling: 0–4 whenever he has played 18+ 4.0. At 3.5 he is 10–1. He beats his level and loses above it — exactly what a strong 3.5 singles player looks like.

Stuart Friess is a genuine second option at 9–8 and 3–0 at 40+ 3.5. If they need two singles courts across a weekend they have them.

How to Play Them
  • Their D3 is the softest identified court all weekend. Whoever they put there comes from Becker / Kamat / Holman / Bohn — 38, 28, 27, 25 percent. Do not overspend on it; win it with your fourth-best pair and bank the point.

  • Singles is a genuine contest. Ivanov at 67% against our best at 67% is a coin flip; against anyone else of ours it is not.
