The pairing they have never made

Across every court on their own schedule, Chris Acker (12–2, 86%) and Brandon Vasholz (21–6, 78%) have never been partnered. Acker has played with Munjewar and with players staying home; Vasholz with Gardner, Wang, Ebert, Hernandez and Dixon.

If Penland puts them together at Sectionals it is the strongest pair in the field and we have no evidence about it at all. If he does not, both are diluted by a weaker partner — which is what has happened in all 24 doubles courts on file.

How to Play Them
  • Concede nothing at S1 but expect to lose it. Hernandez at 88% against our best singles player at 67% is not a court to build a plan around. Spend your strength on the three doubles courts.
  • Find out early whether Acker and Vasholz are paired. If they are, that court is likely gone and the other two are winnable. If they are split, every doubles court is live.
  • Their D2 and D3 are interchangeable. There is no sacrifice court to hunt; put your pairs out in your own order and do not try to duck theirs.
