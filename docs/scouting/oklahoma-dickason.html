How to Play Them
  • Do not plan around a weak singles court. Assume Keith Moore, and assume he is good.
  • There is no soft court to target. This is the flattest roster you face; the win has to come from your own top two courts being better than theirs, not from finding a weak link.
  • Their top pair is beatable. Dickason and Richardson are 1–2 together and 57% each. If they lead with that pair, D1 is winnable.
